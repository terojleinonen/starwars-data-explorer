## Exact final prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: original cinematic background artwork for the Films collection of an independent Star Wars universe website. Primary request: a sweeping planetary dawn seen from orbit, richly atmospheric, photorealistic cinematic matte painting. Wide landscape composition, approximately 16:9. The upper left 55 percent must remain almost black open space with very sparse tiny stars, providing calm negative space behind the website heading and filters. On the right a huge dark rocky planet curves from lower left to upper right; a warm pale golden sun barely emerges over the planet's rim in the right third, an exceptionally thin luminous amber atmosphere. Faint distant dusty nebula in the top right and a tiny dark moon, restrained and believable. Lower half of image is dark planet night side fading towards black, no distracting foreground details. Color palette charcoal, muted bronze, warm ivory sunlight, black space, restrained and premium, photographic lens, subtle film texture but clean enough for compressed web background. Designed to sit behind a dark catalogue layout while preserving text readability. Original generic celestial bodies only, no recognizable franchise planet, ship, character, logo, lettering, UI, border or watermark. Do not imitate a movie poster; deliver only the unlabelled scene.
