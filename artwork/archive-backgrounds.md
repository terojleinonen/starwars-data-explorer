## Encoding and delivery

Each original is resized to 800, 1200 and 1672 px width with existing Sharp: AVIF quality 48 / effort 6; WebP quality 74 / effort 5. The decorative server-rendered picture selects one local responsive variant per page. No animation, video, new dependency or external image service is added. Originals stay outside the public directory.

The shared scene colors and shading keep the existing layout and content intact. Record pages use stronger shading for longer text; mobile shading is tuned separately.

AI generation does not itself guarantee copyright protection or legal clearance. The prompts deliberately avoid identifiable franchise characters, ships, logos and particular fictional locations.
