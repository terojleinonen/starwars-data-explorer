## Kohtaus- ja identiteettirajaukset

### Entombed

All five fugitive squad members have physical scenes; Hunter is individually confirmed in inspected released stills 3 and 16, not inferred from a generic Batch label. Still 3 also shows the arrival craft. Phee and MEL are present. Cid is not certified from ownership of the parlor or its Related card alone.

MEL-221’s body is destroyed, but the memory backup survives and its own main profile explicitly describes transfer to a MEL-222 body. Keep one droid history; the later rebuilt body is not physically shown in this selected episode review. GNK-like legs or astromech resemblance do not identify Gonky or a new GNK model.

Skara Nal is the barren destination; its ancient entrance, moving structure, Heart of the Mountain and compass are different objects/site levels. The exact giant-machine taxonomy, maker, age and creature species remain unestablished. Phee’s ancient-power story is not an independently verified history of the Jedi or an exact date.

Falcon compressor, Luke’s compass, Indiana Jones and the older film comparisons are design/history references, not current visits or shared object identity. Vadnay is a proposed future trip, not a visited setting. Full credits, releases and register unchanged.

### Tribe

The five squad members, Gungi, Yanna and Venomor have actual scenes. Wrecker is individually identified by trivia 4’s dialogue and the existing guide’s hero image description. Gungi’s explicit current own profile calls Kashyyyk his homeworld; his unknown village is not a second named birth settlement or proof he is Yanna’s biological child.

Vanguard Axis is a droid organisation, not one person/species. Its own name and the reversed Axis Vanguard spelling in Gungi’s profile refer to the same cartel here. Ordo Tera is the named spaceport, not Ordo Moon. The existing Wookie species record’s inherited spelling remains; no duplicate taxonomy.

Netcasters and mylayas are explicitly named current creatures. Wyyyschokk was an unused idea, and kinrath supplied design inspiration; neither becomes a current species row. The unfinished Clone Wars arc, Wrecker’s recollection and the writer’s history do not create a released episode or certify every older scene.

Trandoshan hunters and clone troops are different participants. Imperial tanks and flame weapons are not individually certified as an AAT, corporate tank droid, particular flamethrower model or TK recruitment type. Gungi’s calm with the netcasters does not make all members harmless in every situation; Venomor being trapped is not a newly invented exact death date. No full credit/register/date expansion.

### The Clone Conspiracy

This installment’s current strand is Coruscant, Rex and the Senate; no Hunter, Tech, Wrecker, Echo or Omega scene row from the programme title. Cade and Slip are distinct witnesses. Their memories of Tipoca City do not add them to an earlier episode roster or make Kamino a current physically visited setting.

Mas Amedda is explicitly part of the present narrative in caption 4, but its released image shows Chuchi at the cantina, not the Amedda/Rampart exchange. Preserve representation as unspecified for that selected source rather than inventing a physical-versus-remote observation. Palpatine is discussed, not certified physically present; the next episode’s trivia separately names his first bodily appearance.

Tynnra Pamlo is named by trivia 3 and shown as the current animated senator in its inspected large image; Rogue One inset is historical. Representation of former corporations does not put San Hill or Gunray in the chamber. The Commerce Guide wording is not used to create a new organisation or verify every corporate history.

The sniper is an actual clone, revealed when Rex removes the helmet in 14. Trivia 9 explicitly says no name in this episode’s credits. The current generic Clone X main profile/Related cards do not independently provide a named code or exact individual identity, so this record remains an unidentified Coruscant assassin, not every later CX trooper or a claimed known clone.

The Martez sisters own the garage (trivia 7); ownership and Decommissioned history do not certify either sister’s current body. The updated guide’s later CX laboratory, doctors, ships and trooper cards are not scene proof. All selected contexts, no full cast/end-credit inventory.

### Truth & Consequences

All five squad members, Rex, Chuchi, Organa, Burtoni, Rampart, Amedda and Palpatine are current physical participants. The Emperor’s bodily presence is explicit in narrative 11–12 and trivia 2; do not reuse the prior episode’s mere discussion as a physical appearance. First guide message and later in-person Rex use one direct row with current body evidence.

The unidentified assassin appears as remains in a body bag in caption 2, not a living visitor, resurrection or a newly assigned clone code. Slip is mentioned but not certified as a new body/recording scene. Burtoni’s former seat is not a current elected office.

Rampart’s docked operational ship holds the backup logs. Keep a ship-instance record distinct from the general Republic/Venator class and the Bracca wreck; selected captions do not alone certify the exact hull name, class or technical numbers. V-wings are explicitly actual air support in 7, unlike the earlier study schematic. Generic gunships, lift and escape pod models remain unclassified.

Tipoca City’s destruction is replayed as evidence in 10. Its setting row is marked transmission with an archival-footage note, not a current visit, a fresh bombardment or proof all of Kamino was destroyed. Do not assign every clone witness or specific hull to the footage. Palpatine turns the accusation into support for his military policy; Rampart’s arrest is not death.

Echo chooses to accompany Rex after a physical farewell in 14; he is alive and absent from the following mining-party strand within this selected scope. Lula is a toy, not another living character. The secure Level 1313 building is not automatically the earlier named garage. Full credits, dates and existing episode cohorts retained.

### The Crossing

Hunter, Wrecker, Tech and Omega form the current mining party. Echo is missed after joining Rex, not certified dead, physically on Ipsidon or remotely contacted. Crosshair is not inferred from the squad name. Tech’s own description of processing emotions differently is not a new psychiatric diagnosis.

Benni is the current observer shown in the inspected large image of trivia 1, alongside an older Elan comparison inset. Early development considered Balosar; that unused option does not verify the released character’s species, make him Elan or create a second person. His later Mokkotown history is only introductory context, not a second episode review.

Cid is contacted remotely through the abandoned town’s array in 17; mark transmission, not her physical presence on Ipsidon or automatic Ord Mantell setting. Roland and Millegi are discussed retrospectively (trivia 3), not current body scenes. Related Gonky, Mokko, later doctors and laboratory cards do not establish appearances.

Ipsidon, its mines, abandoned spaceport and Mokkotown are distinct levels. The named buck herd stampedes ahead of the storm; no average size or full taxonomy invented. Ipsium is a mineral, not a species, ship or person. Missing the Marauder and exploding one ore container do not mean the ship, world or all available ore is destroyed. Full credits, releases and register unchanged.

Entombedin kuvissa 2 ja 10 Phee/Omega ja sivulla näkyvät ryhmän osat eivät yksin ratkaisseet Hunterin identiteettiä; kuvat 3 ja 16 näyttävät hänet tunnistettavasti. Kuvan 8 ryhmästä ei päätelty nimetöntä luolaeläimen lajia. The Clone Conspiracyn kuva 4 näyttää Chuchin kloonien kanssa eikä sen samassa kuvatekstissä mainittua Amedda/Rampart-vaihtoa: jälkimmäisen esitystapa jätettiin avoimeksi. Nykyiset Pamlo- ja Benni-kuvat erotettiin vanhoista inseteistä. Triben Wreckerillä on trivian nimetty repliikki ja aiemman virallisen oppaan hero-kuvan suora nimikuvaus.
