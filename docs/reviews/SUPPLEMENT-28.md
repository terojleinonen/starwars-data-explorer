Omega’s relative age is stated in trivia 1; no numerical birth year or universal clone-aging rule is added. Generic landing craft and tank references do not certify LAAT, AAT or TK-recruit models. Full credits and dates unchanged.

Hunter and Echo are explicitly offworld in caption 1; no physical scene row is inferred from their discussion. The current participants are Tech, Wrecker, Omega, Cid, TAY-0, Millegi, Venim and Kane. Other racers and crowd species remain outside the selected cohort, including Bosco’s naming/design trivia.

Safa Toma is a city on Serolonis, not a separate planet. The racing track is described with the city; its tunnel nickname mentioning Lotho Minor is not a visit to that world. The unrealized Cad Bane/Boba arc and species comparisons with Clegg Holdfast/Force Awakens are historical/design context, not current appearances.

Tech removes weapons for one race, not every riot speeder’s standard configuration. Millegi’s species is not a moral rule for all Dowutins. Ben Schwartz voice history does not put BB-8 here.
